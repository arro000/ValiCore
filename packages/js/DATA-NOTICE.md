# Geographic dataset attribution and limits

Project MIT licensing does not relicense third-party datasets/documents.

Sources: Ministero dell'Interno, ANPR municipality archive and foreign decoding
tables; Istat, administrative lists and Sardinian variations; Agenzia delle
Entrate, T2 province labels; reviewed supplementary geographic evidence.

ANPR: https://www.anagrafenazionale.interno.it/note-legali/

Istat: https://www.istat.it/note-legali/

Their site notices state Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 unless otherwise
specified, including source-specific/third-party exceptions:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

ValiCore normalizes/reconciles the supplied editions into dated JSON and derives
evidence-linked administrative overlays. Original URLs, hashes and reference
dates are preserved in dataset provenance. No provider endorses ValiCore. The
catalog does not prove tax-registry allocation or universally complete history.

**ADE T2/publication reuse terms remain under review.** Public consultability
is not a redistribution grant; no CC-BY or MIT license is assigned by analogy.
Publication requires the documented owner go/no-go review of this residual issue.
