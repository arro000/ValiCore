# Code of Conduct

ValiCore welcomes constructive participation regardless of experience, identity,
background or ability. This policy applies to project issues, discussions, pull
requests and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Accept technical feedback and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples rather than real personal, fiscal or
  health data, and never publish someone's credentials or private correspondence.
- Keep discussion relevant and accessible; do not impersonate contributors or
  claim official endorsement for this independent project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and
sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, request a change in behavior,
lock a discussion, or restrict participation according to the severity and
context. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistently applied.

For non-sensitive public conduct, ask the maintainer to moderate the relevant
thread without repeating personal or abusive material. For violations of GitHub's
platform policies, use GitHub's reporting/support tools. The project does not
currently operate a separate private conduct-reporting mailbox; do not publish
private incident details in an issue to compensate for that limitation.

Vulnerability reports follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
